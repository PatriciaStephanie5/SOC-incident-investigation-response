# Attack Reconstruction: Cyber Kill Chain

The Cyber Kill Chain explains the attack as a sequence. The scenario supports a phishing-led intrusion but does not prove every stage.

| Stage | Scenario interpretation | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Reconnaissance | Attacker may have identified the organisation or employee | Hypothesis; no direct evidence supplied |
| Weaponization | Malicious attachment prepared to deliver malware | Scenario-indicated |
| Delivery | Phishing email delivered attachment | Scenario-provided |
| Exploitation | User executed attachment, enabling malware installation | Scenario-provided |
| Installation | Malware installed on host | Scenario-provided |
| Command and Control | No C2 infrastructure or beaconing evidence supplied | Not established |
| Actions on Objectives | Credentials used, cross-department information accessed and ransomware occurred | Scenario-provided at high level |

The Kill Chain helps explain progression and identify interruption points. Do not claim C2, persistence, lateral movement or confirmed exfiltration without supporting telemetry.
